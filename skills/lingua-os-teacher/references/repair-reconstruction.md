# Repair and reconstruction playbook

Load this playbook after an assessed failure, an observation-practice selection, an active deliberate weakness, or an open reconstruction requirement. A recurring natural observation routed as `observation_diagnostic` must receive its cold deliberate diagnostic first; do not teach the suspected answer before that diagnostic. Keep any later repair on the already-selected objective.

## Diagnose before reteaching

Distinguish the cheapest plausible cause:

- **slip:** learner likely knows the mechanism and made a local execution error;
- **production-control gap:** recognition/explanation may be intact but unaided production is weak;
- **model gap:** learner is applying a coherent but wrong rule or distinction;
- **wording/working-memory problem:** the challenge presentation is obscuring the target;
- **ASR/modality uncertainty:** the source cannot support the claimed learner error.

A production miss is not proof that rule knowledge is missing.

## Repair ladder

Use the smallest step that can close the demonstrated gap:

```text
re-contextualize the same target
-> simplify the task while preserving the target
-> one discriminating hint or contrast
-> minimum explicit explanation
-> learner reconstruction
```

Rules:

- Target the exact error that occurred, not a nearby curriculum concept.
- Do not keep asking Socratic questions after inference has stopped producing useful information.
- A neutral rephrase is not automatically a hint. If the rephrase reveals solution structure, record it through the normal hint lifecycle before showing it.
- If a material correction reveals the answer or missing mechanism, record exposure before reveal with the existing correction/exposure API.
- Immediate corrected repetition after answer exposure is guided/exposed, not independent.
- Require reconstruction when the correction supplied a meaningful missing mechanism; do not require ceremonial reconstruction for optional style polish.
- Later independent evidence must come from a changed frozen surface selected through normal Lingua planning.

## Error-to-future-practice discipline

Do not create prompt-local retrieval cards or a second scheduler. The repair may identify what needs later retrieval, but the existing evidence, ReviewRatingMapper, FSRS, weakness, and planning layers own when and why it returns.
