# Assumption Inversion

Use this method when the current approach seems inevitable because its assumptions have gone unquestioned.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the current approach and what feels locked in.
2. Extract 5-8 load-bearing assumptions across several categories:
   - user
   - value
   - channel
   - sequence
   - price
   - effort split
3. Flip each assumption into its strongest plausible opposite. Do not turn it into absurdity; a reasonable competitor should be able to defend the flip.
4. For every flip, ask: `Where is this already true? Who profits from it today?`
5. Name a real market, segment, era, behavior, or product where the flipped assumption holds. If no honest example exists, mark the flip dead and keep it visible.
6. Develop the 2-3 live flips into concrete directions. Borrow mechanisms from places where the flip already works.
7. Scan the surviving flips for a shared hidden axis or belief. State the meta-pattern.
8. Rank the survivors, keep dead flips visible, and offer next moves.

## Honesty mechanics

At least one flip often dies. If every flip works, inspect whether the flips were too weak or the examples were manufactured.

A dead flip can be valuable because it identifies a genuine constraint that should not consume more creative effort.

## Prohibitions

- Do not flip into cartoon absurdity; use Provocation for that.
- Do not stop at the opposite statement. The `where is this true?` hunt is the load-bearing step.
- Do not flip more than eight assumptions in one pass.
